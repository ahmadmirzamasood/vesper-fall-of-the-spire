HOW TO PLAY

Defeat Acheron. The mission ends automatically after the boss destruction sequence.

W A S DMoveW forward, S back, A left, D right.
SPACE + MOVEDash / evadeHold a movement key and tap Space for a burst of speed.
HOLD F / CLICKFire arc rifleHold F or the left mouse button over the arena. Targeting is automatic.
QOverdriveFire a powerful core discharge. Recharges in 12 seconds.
ESCPause / resumePause the action at any time during the encounter.
HOpen this guideThe game pauses while you read.

SURVIVE THE ENCOUNTER

  1. Keep moving. Leave the orange ground circle before the strike lands.
  2. Manage heat. Release fire to cool down. At critical heat the rifle locks until it vents.
  3. Watch phase two. Below half boss health, strikes become faster and cover a larger area.

SOUND toggles audio. QUALITY switches High / Medium; choose Medium for smoother play. REDEPLOY starts a fresh encounter after a win or defeat.
