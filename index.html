HOW TO PLAY

Clear every hostile frame in the mission. After the final destruction sequence, collect scrap, upgrade your loadout, and deploy to the next platform.

GAMEPAD · STANDARD MAPPING

Left stick: move · Right stick: manual aim · RT: fire · A / Cross: dash · RB / R1: overdrive · Start: pause · Y / Triangle: guide. In menus, D-pad up/down moves focus and A selects.

MOBILE / TOUCH

Use the on-screen direction pad to move. Hold FIRE; tap DASH while moving or tap OVERDRIVE. In manual mode, drag on the arena to aim. Use the pause button to stop the action.

SURVIVE THE ENCOUNTER

  1. Keep moving. Leave the orange ground circle before the strike lands.
  2. Manage heat. Release fire to cool down. At critical heat the rifle locks until it vents.
  3. Watch phase two. Below half boss health, strikes become faster and cover a larger area.

SOUND toggles audio. QUALITY switches High / Medium; choose Medium for smoother play. REDEPLOY starts a fresh encounter after a win or defeat.
